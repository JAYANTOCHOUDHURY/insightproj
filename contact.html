04 · Common questions

Things people ask us first.

Can't find your answer? Just ask us directly — we'd rather answer than assume.

What kinds of products do you photograph? +

Everything from small consumer goods and apparel to food, beverages, cosmetics, electronics and B2B industrial products. If you can ship it, we can photograph it.

Where are you located, and do you travel? +

Our studio is in Bentonville, Arkansas. We serve brands throughout Northwest Arkansas — Bentonville, Bella Vista, Rogers, Lowell, Springdale, Fayetteville and beyond. Travel for larger projects is available on request.

How long does a typical project take? +

Most product shoots are delivered within 3–7 business days after the shoot. Larger catalog work or full content campaigns typically run 2–4 weeks. We'll give you a clear timeline in your quote.

Do you offer ongoing content partnerships? +

Yes. Many of our clients work with us on a monthly or quarterly basis for ongoing product photography, lifestyle content and video. Ask us about partnership packages.

Can you handle retouching and file prep too? +

Yes. Retouching, color correction, background cleanup and format-specific crops are included in every quote. You receive files optimized for Amazon, Shopify, Etsy, Instagram, print and web.

What about branding, web and print design? +

Through our sister company, Capik Creative, we offer graphic design, branding, web design, copywriting and print materials. This means you can get everything from photography to branding under one creative umbrella.
