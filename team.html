Meet the team
Anchoring Our Story in Bentonville

Where creativity and passion converge.

Welcome to Insight Studios, where creativity and passion converge to illuminate the beauty of every moment. Founded by Jamie Caporaso and Bob Caporaso, our studio stands as a beacon of artistic excellence in the heart of Bentonville, Arkansas.

Our journey to this vibrant city was guided by a quest for inspiration and a desire to cultivate a community where artistry thrives. From the moment we arrived, we were captivated by Bentonville's warmth, creativity and unwavering sense of belonging.

As proud members of this extraordinary community, we are honored to share our vision and expertise with you. At Insight Studios, we invite you to embark on a journey of discovery and transformation, where every image tells a story and every moment is a masterpiece.

02 · Founder story

Jamie Caporaso

Co-founder · Photographer

With over 30 years of visual expertise, Jamie brings a rich background in corporate photography from her time at Pfizer Inc. to Insight Studios.

She is a seasoned design professional with a flair for storytelling and a keen eye for detail.

Drawn to Northwest Arkansas by the vibrant art scene and the allure of Crystal Bridges, as well as the thriving bike culture, Jamie finds inspiration in her surroundings.

Jamie is known for always having a smile and consistently going above and beyond for clients, ensuring their needs are met with creativity and dedication. At Insight Studios, Jamie is committed to collaborating closely with clients, using her experience to help them navigate and stand out in the ever-evolving digital landscape.

03 · Founder story

Bob Caporaso

Co-founder · Visual Content Expert

A practical and efficient visual content expert at Insight Studios, Bob is dedicated to delivering exactly what clients want and need.

He leads our team in creating captivating visuals that elevate retail presence, ensuring your brand stands out in the digital landscape with product photography, lifestyle imagery, infographics, video and 360º imagery.

Outside the studio, you can catch Bob hitting the trails on his bike or getting his hands dirty with trail maintenance — showcasing the same dedication and finesse he brings to his work.
