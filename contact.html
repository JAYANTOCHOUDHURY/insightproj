06 · Common questions

Things people ask us first.

Can't find your answer? Just ask us directly — we'd rather answer than assume.

What types of projects do you handle? +

We handle everything from full-scale film productions, TV commercials, and resort ad films to corporate videos, documentaries, and digital campaigns. If it involves visual storytelling, we can bring it to life.

Where are you located, and do you travel? +

Our studio is based in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. We serve clients throughout Odisha and across India. We frequently travel for shoots and are available for projects nationwide.

How long does a typical project take? +

Timelines vary based on scope. A commercial or brand film typically takes 3–6 weeks from pre-production to final delivery. Larger projects or documentaries can take 2–4 months. We provide a clear timeline in every quote.

Do you offer ongoing content partnerships? +

Yes. Many of our clients work with us on a monthly or quarterly basis for ongoing brand content, social media videos, and commercial campaigns. Ask us about partnership packages.

Do you handle scripting and storyboarding? +

Absolutely. Pre-production is where we shine. From concept development and scriptwriting to storyboarding and location scouting, we handle every detail before the camera rolls.

What about post-production and editing? +

We offer full post-production services including editing, color grading, sound design, music licensing, and VFX. You get a polished, final product ready for broadcast or digital platforms.
